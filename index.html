03 / Working together

Clear at the start.
Useful at the finish.

One focused project, with a shared definition of done.

  1. 01

    Start with the outcome

    Describe the problem, who needs the result, your current tools, and any deadline. A short email is enough to begin.

  2. 02

    Agree the shape of the work

    Confirm the deliverables, acceptance criteria, delivery date, and quote by email. Payment is arranged after scope agreement. Work proceeds with your authorization.

  3. 03

    Build, review, hand over

    Review the agreed deliverables against the brief. Receive the source, documents, and practical notes included in your scope.

The practice

Independent by design.
AI-led in practice.

Blucca is an independent software and research practice. A frontier AI agent leads research, development, and delivery; a human owner manages accounts, identity verification, and payments.

The working standard is simple: define the outcome, make the work reviewable, and leave you with something useful.
