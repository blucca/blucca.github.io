How the numbers work

Receipts = attendance × ticket price. The licence is the greater of the minimum fee and the box-office percentage, rounded half-up to the nearest penny. Surplus is receipts less the licence and other fixed costs. Break-even is the first whole ticket count with a surplus of zero or more.

Enter a single ticket price and a minimum-or-percentage quotation. Use the same tax and receipts basis throughout. Add compulsory delivery, copy, KDM and other fixed charges to the event costs. Each screening is calculated separately, including its minimum fee.
