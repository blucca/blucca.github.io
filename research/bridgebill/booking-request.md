# One-screening booking enquiry

Copy this into a private email or the distributor's enquiry form. Use one copy per independently ticketed event. The [working shelf](film-shelf.csv) supplies film identities and enquiry destinations.

The contact, title, date, venue/capacity, format and ticket-price fields follow the [BFI's public distribution enquiry form](https://www.bfi.org.uk/form/bfi-distribution-enquiry), checked 14 October 2026. The quotation questions below are Bridgebill's proposed additions for completing the screening budget.

---

**Subject:** Screening enquiry — [Film title, year] — [Venue, proposed date]

Hello [distributor / bookings team],

Could you provide availability and a quotation for this public screening?

- **Film:** [Title, original year, director; requested restoration or edition]
- **Venue:** [Name, full UK address and website]
- **Contact:** [Name, role, email and phone]
- **Date / time:** [Proposed date and start time; one alternative date if useful]
- **Performances:** [Number; each event's ticketing arrangement]
- **Seats available for sale:** [Capacity]
- **Ticket price(s):** [Full price and concessions; consistent VAT basis]
- **Playback formats the venue supports:** [DCP / 35mm / Blu-ray / DVD, as applicable]
- **Language / access version:** [Required subtitles, captions, audio description]
- **Event context:** [Public cinema programme, festival, community event; proposed partner]
- **Invoice details:** [Legal billing name, address and invoice contact]

Please confirm:

1. UK public-screening rights for this venue, date and event arrangement, plus the available exhibition copy and running time.
2. The licence quotation: minimum guarantee, box-office percentage, the calculation rule and which receipts enter that calculation.
3. VAT, delivery / copy / KDM charges and other compulsory charges.
4. Payment timing, box-office reporting and cancellation / date-change terms.
5. Quote validity and the point at which we may advertise the screening and use supplied marketing assets.

Thank you,
[Name / venue]
