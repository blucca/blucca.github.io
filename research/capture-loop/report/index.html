Built for shippers, returns operators, and claims reviewers. The full label establishes context. A close-up makes the selected field larger in the captured image. Capture Loop connects those two jobs with a measured next step and a separate, source-linked confirmation.

The practical task: obtain a checkable label field before a claim reviewer proceeds. UPS requests a close-up shipping-label photograph with its tracking number as part of damage-claim documentation.[1] Capture Loop targets this acquisition step; the carrier's full claim process also uses contents, packaging, and supporting records.

CAPTURE LOOP / TECHNICAL REPORT02 / SYSTEM

The image changes
the next action.

Two native-tool loops serve one field task. The full-label loop recovers framing and focus. The detail loop keeps that context, maps the selected field into another original, and requests the next useful close-up or opens a fresh review.

Full-label tool loop

inspect_capture invokes the private Lambda. Four-corner geometry and normalized focus drive request_recapture or prepare_field_review. The next full-label photo carries the preceding request.

Linked-detail tool loop

inspect_field_detail measures correspondence, target coverage, and sampling. request_detail_recapture asks for a specific change; prepare_detail_review opens comparison of both originals and crops.

The reviewer selects the target, declares the same physical document, reads the value, and records whether the detail is easier, the same, or harder to read. The saved confirmation binds the field selection and both source regions. A new detail starts a fresh confirmation and preserves earlier records.

Implementation: native tool executor · session/API · detail registration

CAPTURE LOOP / TECHNICAL REPORT03 / FULL-LABEL VISION

Find the paper edge.
Measure the useful view.

  1. Bound the geometry workload. Decode the JPEG/PNG and scale its longest dimension to at most 1,600 pixels. Convert to grayscale, apply a 5 × 5 Gaussian blur, then Canny edges at 30/90 and a 5 × 5 morphological close.
  2. Generate physical-page candidates. Keep contours covering at least 8% of the reduced image. Approximate at 2.5% of contour perimeter. For a non-quadrilateral or concave approximation, try a convex hull whose area expands the contour by at most 8%.
  3. Check both sides of each boundary. Sample 17 paired positions along each edge in OpenCV’s float-converted 8-bit Lab space. Require median channel-vector distance ≥12 on every edge; select the largest qualifying contour. The fallback uses the largest Otsu bright component above 12% area.
  4. Separate framing from focus. Record boundary contact and actual clearance. Contact tolerance is max(5 pixels, 0.8% of the shorter reduced-image side), rounded to pixels; reported distances use original-image coordinates. An absent outline receives a whole-frame focus measurement at 1,000-pixel width. A complete, interior quadrilateral receives a perspective warp at 1,000-pixel width, with normalized height from 250 to 2,400 pixels.
  5. Measure the page interior. Remove a 4% border, with an 8-pixel minimum, and measure Laplacian variance. Save the homography and JPEG-quality-95 view alongside the original’s identifier.

The GLS paper edge produced a five-corner approximation. The earlier implementation fell back to the white tabletop: 99.82% of frame area and an incorrect full-edge request. The same repair runs inside the deployed Lambda.

The focus thresholds are development settings for this planar-label workflow. The reviewer establishes the readability of the particular field. Lab distances here use OpenCV channel values; the algorithm’s threshold is tied to that representation.

CAPTURE LOOP / TECHNICAL REPORT04 / FIELD DETAIL AND PAIRED EVIDENCE

The selected field
is the task.

A source-scale probe motivated this second loop. At 1280, 640, and 480 px image widths, one Bell photograph reaches whole-page review with focus 717.825 / 154.113 / 59.973. The fixed printed-code region's median candidate glyph height falls to 16 / 8 / 6 source pixels. Field-local focus changes with scale and resampling; the reviewer supplies the reading.

  1. Select and retain source pixels. A box on the perspective view maps through the inverse page homography. field.py saves an axis-aligned PNG slice of the original. The interface offers drag selection, percentage inputs, and 1× / 2× / 4× display.
  2. Register the next original. detail.py uses SIFT with mutual Lowe ratio 0.75 and RANSAC at 3 working pixels. Work images have long edge ≤1600 px, area ≤1.6 MP, and ≤2400 features. Acceptance requires ≥12 distinct inlier locations, ≥40% inlier ratio, and forward/inverse reprojection median ≤1.5 px and P95 ≤3 px.
  3. Check the actual target. Require full target coverage, ≥6 nearby distinct inliers, and ≥50% target coverage by the inlier hull. The complete parameter set also checks spatial distribution. A mapped field with ≥1.2× linear sampling opens detail review; a smaller footprint keeps its mapped crop and requests a closer photo.

Failure handling and human control

  • Inspect, then act on the current ID. One saved next step ends the turn. Full-label review requires the perspective view, four interior corners, clear frame contact, and finite page focus meeting its returned threshold.
  • Detail review has an executable gate. detail-contract.mjs requires matched status, a four-corner field crop, and mapped field area ratio ≥1.44. Low overlap, an omitted target, and low sampling each produce a specific recapture state while retaining the full-label context.
  • The reviewer owns the value. Same-document declaration precedes a detail upload. The final form binds the latest matched detail, field name, both source regions, reviewer ID, reading, and readability assessment. Stale IDs return HTTP 409; new details clear active confirmation.
  • Processing failures stop that attempt. Provider error, output limit, or the four-turn cap removes the attempt's image and trace files. Earlier saved captures and reviews, the attempt count, and operator error logs remain. Successfully processed recapture requests keep their originals.

Workspaces and source control

A secure HTTP-only cookie identifies each hosted browser workspace. Full-label originals, linked details, source crops, reviews, and exported traces belong to that session. The configured retention period is shown in the interface; the delete control removes session materials earlier. Lambda removes its per-invocation image files on exit.

The station closes new captures at its published expiry; saved records remain available through their own session expiry. Details use the same photo-attempt allowance. One photo processes at a time; reads remain available.

Usability and recovery checks

The real HTTPS browser run saved a scripted GLS date, survived reload, then cleared the active review on a new photo. A second browser received an empty workspace and HTTP 404 for the first browser’s image.

The paired-sample browser check used local OpenCV and the rule controller: both sources stayed linked, the readability choice was required, and reload restored the review. Desktop and 390 px layouts recorded zero page errors and horizontal overflow.

CAPTURE LOOP / TECHNICAL REPORT09 / SCOPE, CONTRIBUTIONS, SOURCES

The next result
comes from the operator.

Operating scope: one flat printed label or page. The context needs visible document edges against a contrasting surface. A detail needs the complete target and shared surrounding print or distinctive marks. Planar feature matching supplies correspondence; the operator confirms the intended physical item, reads the field, and assesses readability. Repeated templates, glare, curvature, and very small print are priority field-study cases.

Participation recorded at publication: independent external operators 0; fresh phone recapture sessions 0; independent participant field readings 0. Published uploads and confirmations use scripts. Controlled Bell resampling, natural SmartDoc frames, live native actions, and scripted UI checks retain their individual source roles.

Task-level evaluation

Ask independent shippers or scanning-tool practitioners to photograph the same label again after an instruction. Record phone, lighting, initial image, chosen field, requested change, new original, mapping result, reading, and easier/same/harder assessment. A completed task has a reviewer-readable value linked to the retained sources and a saved confirmation. Report completion, attempts, elapsed time, abandonment, and erroneous review openings per case. The practical outcome is the number of photo exchanges needed before a reviewer can proceed.
