01 / What the authority changed

A later date. Two new references.

The official correction contains three entries. Each sits below alongside the work it could affect.

C1 / Section IV.2.2

Application deadline

2 Dec 2024 · 12:00 GMT

↓ 9 Dec 2024 · 16:00 GMT

The time limit for receipt of tenders or requests to participate moves by 7 days, 4 hours. This case uses the SQ / request-to-participate stage.

C2 / Section III.1.2

Financial standing

The existing annual-turnover wording, including the £250k exclusion threshold, remains.

Added text“Additional minimum requirements as in the SQ guidance document published.”

C3 / Section III.1.3

Technical ability

The requirement for three relevant examples remains.

Added text“and additional professional and technical capability questions as detailed in the SQ and accompanying guidance.”

Compare the original notice with the correction. The referenced SQ and guidance supply the detailed additional requirements.

03 / Make each action reviewable

A useful “why” reaches all the way to sign-off.

A review record should connect the source text, affected task, retained material, and evidence needed to finish the review.

BidDelta’s proposed workflow adds task-version checks and execution receipts to this chain. The product acceptance target is a selective, repeatable update that preserves the valid parts of the plan.

  1. SourceNotice 036312-2024 · III.1.2

    Additional minimum requirements referenced in SQ guidance.

  2. DependencyFinancial qualification → Final sign-off

    The approved task relies on the requirements being current.

  3. Proposed changeReopen financial review; retain existing proof

    Record the applicable guidance version and review its added requirements.

  4. Completion evidenceFile version + reviewer result + task receipt

    Final sign-off resumes when both qualification reviews are complete.
